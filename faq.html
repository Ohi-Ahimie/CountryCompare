Frequently Asked Questions

Why am I seeing question marks?

I get the information for the site from a provider; it wouldn't be possible for me to make the site otherwise. These providers may have incomplete data or suffer outages. The same goes for the currency converter service I use.

I can't find a country I'm looking for.

Check for mispellings and, if possible, try an alternate name of the country. It's also possible that some territories are not in the dataset.

Some country details seem a little off.

This project is built from existing sources whose data I don't control. I've done my best to fix issues and represent cultures as respectfully as I can. With that said, I understand there will be things that I miss. I invite you to inform me and I will do my best to address them. Thanks!
